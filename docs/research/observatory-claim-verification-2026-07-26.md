# Observatory claim-level verification ledger

**Review date:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** The 35 public Observatory profiles not used in Edition 001. The six Edition 001 cases (Suzie Reider, Emma Chamberlain, Pieter Levels, Jane Gilbert, Jony Ive, and Shonda Rhimes) were reviewed separately for that publication.

## Standard used

This review treats each public role description as one or more claims. A current title, former title, founder relationship, and a `created`/role-built flag are separate claims. Verification of one does not verify the others.

- **Verified:** The narrowed public description is directly supported by a current official, organizational, government, university, or first-party source.
- **Corroborated:** The description is supported by reliable attributable reporting and/or multiple consistent sources, but the ideal first-party record was not available.
- **Narrowed:** A broader or more promotional description was replaced with a claim the available evidence can support.
- **Open:** The role-built question remains a research question. A new or unusual title does not by itself establish that the role was designed around the person.

This ledger verifies public biographical/role claims only. It does not establish equity ownership, governance authority, revenue, portability, data rights, psychological traits, construct scores, causation, or endorsement.

## Corrections made to the public roster

Seventeen descriptions were corrected or narrowed in `src/lib/observatory_seed.ts`. The changes remove stale titles, avoid implying unverified companies or ownership, identify the actual parent organization where necessary, and use current official role language.

The role-built flags remain questions on provisional profiles. They must not be converted into documented classifications until the evidence store contains a separately reviewed claim and supporting sources.

## Follow-up research gate

A profile should move from **provisional** to **verified** only when its public evidence packet includes:

1. a dated identity/role claim;
2. a dated organization or venture relationship;
3. a separate review of any role-built flag;
4. contrary or qualifying evidence where material;
5. an explicit list of unknowns;
6. review metadata and a correction route; and
7. no inference from silence about ownership, authority, portability, or revenue.
