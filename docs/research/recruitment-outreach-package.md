## Core invitation

Institutions of One is recruiting adults for remote cognitive interviews about two candidate research instruments: the Ownership Index and Portfolio Professional.

The interview tests the questions. It does not test or score the participant. I will ask participants to answer selected questions and explain what they thought each question meant, what information they used, whether the response options fit, and where the wording missed their circumstances.

I am seeking people across different career stages, work arrangements, creator business models, jurisdictions, income structures, access to professional support, disability or chronic-illness experiences, and care responsibilities.

## LinkedIn

I am recruiting participants for the first cognitive-interview round of Institutions of One.

The study is testing two candidate instruments:

- the Ownership Index, which examines control over audience access, rights, revenue, identity, and business infrastructure;
- Portfolio Professional, which examines how professional capability becomes reusable, portable, adopted, legible, and able to compound across work.

These versions are not scored. The interview is designed to find unclear terms, missing response options, inaccessible assumptions, and questions that confuse structural advantage with the construct being studied.

I am looking for adults with experience creating, licensing, earning from, organizing, documenting, or communicating work, or managing a portfolio career. I especially need variation in career stage, employment structure, creator model, jurisdiction, platform dependence, income model, disability or care context, and access to professional support.

### Body

I have published the candidate questions behind the next Ownership Index and Portfolio Professional instruments. Before either version can be scored, I need to learn how people actually understand and answer each item.

A cognitive interview is different from taking a finished assessment. You may answer selected questions, but the useful material is the explanation around the answer: what you thought the question meant, what examples or records you used, which option fit, what was missing, and whether the item carried assumptions that failed in your circumstances.

I am recruiting adults across different career stages, work arrangements, creator models, jurisdictions, income structures, levels of platform dependence, access to professional support, disability or chronic-illness experiences, and care responsibilities.

Participation is remote and voluntary. You may skip a question, pause, or stop. Recording and de-identified quotation are optional. The interview produces no score, result, diagnosis, ranking, or eligibility decision.
