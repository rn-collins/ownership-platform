# Round 1 cognitive-interview operations package

Version: round1-2026-07-v1  
Status: operationally frozen; recruitment may begin  
Instruments: Ownership Index 0.3.0-candidate.1 and Portfolio Professional 0.2.0-candidate.1  
Scoring: disabled

## Completion standard

Round 1 is not complete until each instrument has at least eight completed interviews, every item has been administered to at least two participants, at least four declared sampling dimensions are represented with remaining gaps recorded, all observed failures are coded, every major or critical failure has a proposed disposition, and every proposed change has linked evidence.

A completion count alone cannot open the gate.

## Session sequence

1. Verify identity against the scheduled participant and study code.
2. Explain that the instrument—not the participant—is being tested.
3. Reconfirm voluntary participation, the right to skip or stop, privacy boundaries, and the absence of scoring.
4. Reconfirm recording and quotation choices separately.
5. Record consent reconfirmation before marking the session complete.
6. Administer assigned items and required probes.
7. Record contextual information only when needed to understand the response process.
8. Close by asking what the instrument failed to ask and whether any question felt inaccessible, unsafe, or structurally unfair.
9. Explain withdrawal and follow-up procedures.
10. Complete coding and quality review without adding facts not supported by the session record.

## Item assignment

The operations tracker must show, by instrument and item:

- participants assigned;
- participants administered;
- completed probes;
- skipped or stopped items;
- reason not administered;
- response-process domains observed;
- highest severity;
- unresolved disposition;
- Round 2 retest requirement.

No item may be represented as sufficiently tested merely because it appeared in a questionnaire.

## Six-domain coding

Use only the registered domains:

- comprehension;
- retrieval;
- judgment;
- response mapping;
- sensitivity;
- accessibility.

Severity:

- none: no material failure;
- minor: friction without changing the intended construct or response;
- major: could change the response or construct interpretation;
- critical: unsafe, inaccessible, structurally invalid, or incapable of supporting the intended claim.

Evidence must distinguish participant language, researcher observation, and researcher inference. Do not convert an inference into a quotation.

## Revision ledger

Each proposed change must contain:

- instrument, source version, target version, and item;
- construct;
- before and proposed after values;
- change type;
- linked evidence-code identifiers;
- response-process failure;
- severity and observed frequency;
- accessibility or structural-fairness effect;
- rationale;
- proposed claim restriction;
- proposer;
- reviewer;
- decision and date.

A revision may be approved only when every linked evidence code exists and belongs to the relevant item. Candidate.2 generation remains blocked until the Round 1 gate is met and the complete approved ledger is authorized.

## Quality review

For each completed session, verify:

- consent was reconfirmed;
- recording use matches permission;
- every code names an item and registered domain;
- evidence is specific enough to audit;
- severity follows the registered rule;
- proposed action does not exceed the evidence;
- identifying details are removed from analytical notes where feasible;
- withdrawals and access needs were honored.

## Round 1 gate meeting

The gate record must include:

- instrument and candidate version;
- completed participant count;
- item administration coverage;
- sampling matrix and gaps;
- failure counts by domain and severity;
- unresolved major and critical failures;
- revision-ledger completeness;
- accessibility and structural-fairness review;
- deviations;
- named decision maker and date.

The permitted decisions are met or not met. Waiver does not satisfy generation or activation requirements.
