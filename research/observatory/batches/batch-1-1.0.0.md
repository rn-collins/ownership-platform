Version: 1.0.0  
Prepared: 2026-07-25  
Purpose: establish a balanced, source-rich comparison set after the Issa Rae flagship workflow.

## Design

Batch 1 contains five creator cases and five professional cases already present in the public roster. Issa Rae is the completed reference case and is not counted among the ten remaining cases.

The batch is purposive, not representative. It is designed to test the Observatory evidence architecture across consumer products, technology, platforms, media, venture capital, creator-economy institutions, role creation, and research entrepreneurship. No prevalence estimate may be made from this batch.

## Coverage matrix

Each case package must answer or explicitly mark missing:

1. Identity and stable case identifier.
2. Founder, owner, employee, partner, investor, or adviser relationships.
3. Legal or publicly stated ownership structure.
4. Assets and intellectual property publicly attributable to the person or organization.
5. Revenue, funding, valuation, or financial claims, with strict source and language limits.
6. Distribution and platform relationships.
7. Team, management, production, or talent infrastructure.
8. Dated career and organizational events.
9. Role-creation evidence, including what existed before the person entered the role.
10. Portability evidence: what could plausibly continue across employers or platforms.
11. Counterevidence and dependencies.
12. Construct mappings with method labels.
13. Missing evidence and permissible-language restrictions.
14. Public-status decision and accountable reviewer.
15. Versioned package manifest and immutable dossier path.

## Publication gates

A package cannot be published unless:

- every public claim has at least one linked source;
- material ownership claims use a primary source or are qualified as reported claims;
- every public relationship and event has a linked source;
- dates distinguish event dates from webpage observation dates;
- company mission statements are not represented as outcome evidence;
- role novelty is not inferred from a job title alone;
- financial values are withheld when the underlying record is unavailable;
- psychological attributes remain unmeasured unless a named, appropriate method supports them;
- contradictions and material dependencies are visible;
- the package manifest is stored in `ObservatoryReviewPackage`;
- an authenticated researcher approves the package.
