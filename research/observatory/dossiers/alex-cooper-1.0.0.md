## Case definition

This dossier reconstructs Alexandra Cooper's public media system across Call Her Daddy, Barstool Sports, Spotify, SiriusXM, Trending, Unwell Audio Network, Unwell programming and production ventures. It separates Cooper's personal brand and creative authority from entity ownership, platform rights, collaborator labor, reported contract values and enterprise economics.

Proposed public headline: **Host, executive producer and cofounder operating across a flagship podcast, a multi-show network and licensed platform programming.**

Role-built flag: `false`. The evidence supports founder and creator activity, not an established institution designing a unique employment role around Cooper.

## Identity resolution

Alexandra Cooper in this case is the U.S. podcast host who co-created Call Her Daddy with Sofia Franklyn, later became its sole host, and launched Trending and Unwell with Matt Kaplan. The case does not merge her assets with Kaplan's ACE Entertainment interests, Unwell talent's shows, Barstool or platform-owned rights, or unrelated people named Alex Cooper.

## Atomic claims

1. Cooper and Sofia Franklyn co-created Call Her Daddy; later sole-host status does not erase Franklyn's co-creation or Barstool's historical contractual and IP role.
2. Spotify announced that new and existing Call Her Daddy episodes would become exclusive to Spotify in July 2021. Exclusivity was a licensed platform relationship, not Spotify ownership of Cooper or proof of her underlying IP ownership. (AC-S01)
3. Spotify built additional platform programming around Cooper, including After Hours on Spotify Live. That extension depended on Spotify's product and distribution infrastructure. (AC-S02)
4. Spotify identified Call Her Daddy as its second-most-popular global podcast in both 2021 and 2022. Those are platform-defined, time-bounded rankings, not audited audience totals or sole-causation findings. (AC-S03, S04)
5. Cooper and Matt Kaplan launched Trending together in 2023. Public cofounder labels do not disclose equity percentages, voting arrangements, board composition or individual economics. (AC-S05)
6. SiriusXM describes Unwell Audio Network, LLC as a Trending subsidiary launched by Cooper and Kaplan. “Alex Cooper's Unwell Network” is branding shorthand, not proof that Cooper personally owns 100 percent. (AC-S06)
7. SiriusXM obtained specified exclusive global audio and video advertising-sales rights for Call Her Daddy and Unwell's roster, alongside development and distribution obligations beginning in 2025. Creative visibility must remain separate from platform rights and operational dependencies. (AC-S06)
8. The SiriusXM agreement has been reported as worth up to approximately $125 million over three years. The public record reviewed does not establish guaranteed compensation, conditions, revenue sharing, allocations among entities, expenses, taxes or Cooper's net receipt. (AC-S07, S08)
9. Unwell On Air and Unwell Music include hosts, producers, programmers and SiriusXM infrastructure beyond Cooper. Curation or executive-producer credit cannot be converted into sole labor or sole ownership. (AC-S09, S10, S15)
10. Unwell expanded into video, creative-agency, live-event and production activities through separate products and counterparties. A shared brand does not establish a single legal entity, uniform ownership or identical IP terms. (AC-S11, S12)
11. Public reporting documents talent departure and IP-rights questions involving Unwell shows. Network distribution does not by itself establish permanent network ownership of talent-created formats or back catalogs. (AC-S13)
12. Public reporting in 2026 described employee turnover and workplace allegations. Allegations, responses and adjudicated facts must remain separately attributed; neither accusations nor brand success establish Cooper's psychology. (AC-S14)
13. No reviewed public record establishes Cooper's current equity in Trending or Unwell, voting control, board majority, complete entity tree, Call Her Daddy IP schedule, trademark ownership, platform-account ownership, show-master ownership, contract guarantees, profit, personal wealth or current enterprise value.
14. No reviewed source validly measures Cooper's motivation, resilience, cognition, attachment, personality or other psychological constructs. Public performance and first-person narrative are not psychometric evidence.

## Provenance and contradiction controls

Spotify and SiriusXM releases are counterparty statements and constitute two institutional clusters, not independent confirmation of every commercial claim. Unwell, Trending and Cooper-controlled statements form a founder/issuer cluster. Reported $60 million, $100 million or $125 million figures conflict across publications and may reflect estimates, maxima, different scopes or shorthand; none should be represented as Cooper's verified cash compensation. “Founder,” “CEO,” “owner,” “host” and “executive producer” describe different relationships and cannot be substituted for one another.
