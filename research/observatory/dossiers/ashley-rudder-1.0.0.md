## Case definition

This dossier reconstructs Ashley Rudder's public role history across M·A·C Cosmetics, Lashify, Whalar, Deutsch New York, SociStudio, HAUS OF SÔS and her personal creator practice. It tests role-construction and "first" claims while separating appointment, remit, organizational resources, team labor, intellectual property, ownership, performance claims and psychology.

Proposed public headline: **Creator-economy executive whose employers created company-specific Chief Creator Officer roles with distinct teams, remits and institutional dependencies.**

Role-built flag: `true`. Whalar's evidence supports an internally proposed and newly created executive role; Deutsch NY later created its own first holder role. This flag does not establish global firstness, personal ownership, sole creation or causal outcomes.

## Identity resolution

Ashley Rudder is the marketing, beauty and creator-economy executive whose career includes M·A·C, Lashify, Whalar and Deutsch NY. This case does not merge her with similarly named people. It does not treat Whalar, Whalar Group, Deutsch NY, SociStudio, Attivo Group, M·A·C, Estée Lauder Companies, Lashify, HAUS OF SÔS or Rudder's personal media accounts as one enterprise.

## Atomic claims

1. Estée Lauder Companies identified Rudder as M·A·C Director, Digital Content & Artistry in 2020. That employee title does not establish ownership of M·A·C, its channels, content, products or results. (AR-S07, S14)
2. Rudder and later profiles describe a career progression from makeup artistry to digital-content leadership at M·A·C. The record supports role evolution but does not disclose internal job descriptions, budgets, reporting lines or sole authorship of the function. (AR-S06, S07)
3. Rudder joined Whalar in September 2021 as North American Head of Creator Partnerships. Whalar existed before her arrival and already operated creator-marketing services. (AR-S01, S02)
4. In her attributed account, Rudder said she proposed expanded creator support and management later created a Global Chief Creator Officer role, which she began in December 2022. This supports role construction at Whalar, not universal firstness. (AR-S02)
5. Whalar publicly called Rudder the industry's first Global Chief Creator Officer. That is an employer publicity claim; no worldwide title registry or exhaustive predecessor search establishes the universal negative. (AR-S01)
6. Whalar described a remit spanning creator sourcing, negotiations, contracts, consultation and performance analysis, supported by a global team and third-party or proprietary data systems. Executive remit is delegated institutional authority, not personal ownership or sole labor. (AR-S01, S02, S08)
7. Rudder reported leading a team that grew from five to 30. The figures are attributed, time-bounded and do not establish direct-report composition, employment status, budget, productivity or sole causal responsibility. (AR-S02)
8. Deutsch NY appointed Rudder Chief Creator Officer in July 2024, overseeing creative across DNY and SociStudio. Muse described her as the first holder at that agency, not the first worldwide. (AR-S03–S05)
9. DNY launched SociStudio with Tom Lyons as president while appointing Rudder over the creative offering. The studio therefore has distinct leadership and institutional formation evidence; it cannot be treated as Rudder's solely founded or owned business. (AR-S03)
10. In Rudder's early Deutsch interview, she said DNY already had a content shop and that her first work involved listening, hypotheses and client calls. This contradicts shorthand that the agency built an entire creator operation from nothing around her. (AR-S05)
11. HAUS OF SÔS is publicly described as a co-founded creative collective. Co-founder status does not establish present entity status, equal equity, unilateral control, IP ownership, revenue or continuing operations. (AR-S06, S11)
12. Rudder's personal portfolio reports campaign, pitch, network and award outcomes. These remain self-reported marketing claims unless matched to client records, award databases, measurement definitions, comparison periods and collaborator credits. (AR-S09, S10)
13. None of the reviewed sources discloses Rudder's equity, voting rights, profit participation, employment contracts, compensation, client-contract economics, platform-account ownership or complete IP schedules across these organizations.
14. None of the reviewed evidence validly measures Rudder's motivation, cognition, resilience, personality, mental state or other psychological constructs.

## Provenance and contradiction controls

Whalar's release, Rudder's as-told-to interview and later biographies share a common employer/subject provenance cluster. Repetitions of "first-ever" are not independent verification when they trace to the same announcement. Deutsch NY and Attivo materials form a second issuer cluster. "First at Whalar," "first at Deutsch NY," "first Global Chief Creator Officer," and "first Chief Creator Officer in advertising" are different claims and may not be substituted for one another.

The evidence supports deliberate role construction at Whalar. It also shows that Whalar, its creator practice, Deutsch NY's content capabilities and SociStudio's multi-executive structure were not created solely around Rudder. "Led," "oversaw," "founded," "co-founded," "built," "created" and "owned" remain distinct.
