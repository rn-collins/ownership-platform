## Identity resolution

Eric “Astro” Teller is a computer scientist, entrepreneur, author and the current Captain of Moonshots at X, Alphabet’s moonshot factory. X states that he is responsible for steering its projects; institutional and fellowship biographies also document earlier co-founding and executive relationships with BodyMedia, Cerebellum Capital, Zivio Technologies and Sandbox Advanced Development. These are separate, time-bounded relationships rather than one continuously owned portfolio.

## Seed hypothesis tested

The map treats “Captain of Moonshots” as an unusually tailored role. The reviewed record establishes Teller as X’s current holder and describes leadership responsibilities, but does not establish worldwide firstness, a formal CEO equivalence in Alphabet filings, or that Teller personally proposed, negotiated or constructed the title. X is a division of Google LLC and describes a factory made up of project teams, inventors, engineers, designers, makers, finance staff, laboratories and outside partners. `roleBuiltFlag=false`.

## Contradictions, adverse evidence and open questions

1. An unusual title is not proof that Teller created or negotiated the role.
2. “Captain of Moonshots” does not establish legal corporate office or CEO equivalence.
3. X expressly rejects the lone-inventor model and attributes work to teams, shared functions and partners.
4. Graduated companies retain separate legal entities, leadership, investors, employees, IP and outcomes.
5. Loon, Makani and Everyday Robots prevent a success-only portfolio account.
6. Alphabet’s Other Bets segment combines businesses and cannot measure X or Teller alone.
7. Reported layoffs and capital-strategy changes are institutional adverse evidence, not psychological evidence.
8. Inventorship does not itself prove patent ownership, licensing rights or commercialization proceeds.
9. Earlier-company co-founding does not prove complete or continuing ownership.
10. Employment terms, compensation, project budgets, cap tables, transaction proceeds, IP allocations, media rights, causal performance and psychology remain unresolved.
