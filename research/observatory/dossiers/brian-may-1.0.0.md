## Identity resolution

Sir Brian Harold May is a musician, songwriter, producer, astrophysicist, stereoscopy researcher and publisher, and wildlife advocate. He is a founding member of Queen, completed an Imperial College London doctorate in astrophysics in 2007, heads the London Stereoscopic Company, and co-founded the Save Me Trust with Anne Brummer. These activities involve distinct bands, companies, coauthors, publishers, scientific institutions, mission teams, charities, trustees, employees, contractors and rights holders. This dossier does not treat the Queen catalogue, Queen companies, compositions, live shows, recordings, name and likeness rights, scientific data, books, patents, charity assets or collaborator output as May's personal property without holder-specific evidence.

## Seed hypothesis tested

The map presents May as a musician, astrophysics PhD and publisher. Public evidence supports all three role families and substantial cross-domain contribution. It does not establish one personally owned “Brian May portfolio.” Queen is a multi-member band with separate corporate and contractual structures; May's science is embedded in scholarly and mission teams; London Stereoscopic Company publications involve coauthors, photographers, publishers and product rights; and Save Me is a separately governed charity co-founded with Anne Brummer.

## Contradictions, adverse evidence, and open questions

1. “Brian May's Queen catalogue” is unsafe shorthand; Queen's catalogue and associated rights involve multiple people, estates, companies, contracts and a reported buyer.
2. Founding-member status does not establish sole formation, ownership of the band name, or ownership of every recording and composition.
3. The 2024 Queen Productions directorship endpoint prevents present-tense corporate-authority claims.
4. The reported £1 billion transaction value is not May's personal proceeds, liquidity or wealth.
5. A doctorate and mission collaboration establish bounded scientific roles, not sole discovery or universal authority.
6. London Stereoscopic Company leadership does not publicly resolve its full cap table, governance, employment, copyright, image or royalty arrangements.
7. Patent inventorship and patent ownership are distinct.
8. Charity co-founding and trusteeship do not create beneficial ownership of charitable assets.
9. Current touring, company, publishing, scientific and charity roles require periodic refresh.
