## Purpose

This dossier tests the seed hypothesis that Publicis Creative U.S. built a social, creator and earned system around Charlotte (“Charlie”) Tansill. It separates company-specific title novelty, delegated remit, pre-existing institutional capability, career history, distributed labor, campaign attribution, ownership, causal performance and psychology.

## Identity resolution

The subject is Charlotte “Charlie” Tansill, the communications and strategy executive who spent approximately 17 years at Ogilvy before joining Publicis Creative U.S. in late 2025. Her first-person announcement, employer materials, professional profile and trade coverage align. No similarly named person was merged.

## Evidence-backed synopsis

Publicis Creative U.S. appointed Tansill President, Social, Creator and Earned, U.S. in November 2025. Contemporaneous reports describe the position as newly created and identify her as its first holder. The reviewed record does not establish worldwide or industry firstness.

The announced remit is to collaborate with leaders and teams across social, creator and earned-first creativity; design integrated offerings; build centers of excellence; support go-to-market work; and advise client leaders. Tansill reports to Publicis Groupe Global Chief Strategy Officer Carla Serrano and joined a broader U.S. leadership team. Those facts establish delegated authority inside a shared system, not ownership or unilateral control.

Publicis’s creator apparatus predates the appointment. Publicis announced its acquisition of Influential in July 2024 and described a creator network, technology, data, expertise and delivery system available across its clients and teams. Epsilon, Publicis agencies, social teams and other acquired capabilities also predate Tansill’s role. The appointment therefore consolidated and developed existing capabilities rather than creating the system from zero.

Tansill’s earlier Ogilvy career included social-practice leadership, Chief Transformation and Strategy Officer in New York, Global Chief Strategy Officer for Ogilvy PR, and President of PR, Social and Influence for North America. Employer and trade materials associate her with practice growth, integrated offerings, awards and campaigns including Michael CeraVe. The same materials identify large teams, clients, creatives, executives, partner agencies and platform contributors. Her role was substantial, but public evidence cannot isolate her individual causal contribution or convert institutional awards and campaign outcomes into personal property.

## Contradictions and adverse-evidence register

1. “First president” is supportable only at Publicis Creative U.S.; no worldwide or industry firstness is established.
2. “Newly created role” describes a title and coordination remit, not newly created creator, social, earned, data, media or agency capabilities.
3. The reporting line and collaboration language contradict unilateral-control shorthand.
4. Publicis’s 2024 Influential acquisition and Epsilon infrastructure predate Tansill’s appointment.
5. Ogilvy’s team-size and collective award language contradict sole-labor and sole-causation framing.
6. Reported campaign impressions, awards and agency rankings are not personal financial outcomes or isolated causal estimates.
7. No material adjudicated dispute directly bearing on the role-construction claim was located in reviewed searches; that negative search is not proof of absence.
