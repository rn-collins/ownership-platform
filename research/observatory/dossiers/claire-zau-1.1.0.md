## Purpose

This version expands the seed package into a role-construction and institutional-boundary review. It tests the proposition that Claire Zau built a novel investor-creator role at Lightspeed while separating firm-specific role design from worldwide firstness, pre-existing assets, delegated authority, institutional ownership, distributed labor, audience and IP rights, deal attribution, contract economics and psychology.

## Evidence-backed synopsis

Lightspeed appointed Zau as Partner & New Media in 2026. Its current profile says she invests at pre-seed and seed, helps shape new-media strategy and co-hosts Lightwork. A Lightspeed executive called her the firm's first investor + creator. The public record therefore supports a newly combined position at Lightspeed. It does not establish that Zau is the first person anywhere in venture capital to combine investing and creator work.

The component capabilities predated the appointment. Zau had invested for about six years at GSV, led AI investment coverage, worked across GSV Ventures and ASU+GSV programming, wrote an AI-and-education newsletter and built a large personal social audience while employed there. Lightspeed already possessed investment, marketing, founder-support, research and media infrastructure. Her present remit is collaborative: she “helps shape” strategy and co-hosts; reporting says her personal accounts remain independent. No reviewed public record discloses her partnership economics, carry, investment-committee vote, employment agreement, content-IP schedule, audience-data rights or unilateral decision authority.

3. “Built Lightspeed's media strategy” conflicts with “helps shape” and named collaborators.
4. Partner status does not disclose equity, carry, votes, employment status or compensation.
5. Audience metrics cannot become owned reach, trust, revenue, deal flow or investment returns.
6. GSV portfolio claims remain institutional unless precise deal attribution is established.
7. No material adjudicated misconduct tied to this role-construction claim was located; negative search is not proof of absence.
