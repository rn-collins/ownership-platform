## 7. Contradiction and uncertainty register

1. **Contrarian Thinking Capital is described inconsistently.** Some company copy calls it a small-business holding company; its own site and regulatory record identify a venture adviser/fund operation.
2. **Contrarian Thinking legal entities are inconsistent.** Current privacy text names Contrarian Thinking, LLC but later references Barefoot Money and Mind Mint, LLC d/b/a Contrarian Thinking.
3. **“Owns dozens” lacks a dated schedule.** Counts vary by interview, site and whether venture minority stakes are included.
4. **Fund ownership differs from portfolio ownership.** A GP/adviser manages fund interests; it does not personally own 100% of each portfolio company.
5. **Co-owner lacks percentage.** ResiBrands supports co-ownership but not stake size, voting rights or direct versus indirect holding.
6. **Company versus person.** “We own” may refer to Sanchez, her spouse, a holdco, a fund, an affiliate or co-investors.
7. **Revenue is self-reported and definition-free.** Nine-figure claims cannot be compared or scored without scope and accounting basis.
8. **Member outcomes are not company acquisitions.** Businesses bought by students or clients must remain outside Sanchez’s portfolio.
9. **Book/media IP is distinct from operating-company equity.**
10. **Pending litigation is not liability.** Complaints and docket entries require procedural labels and later disposition checks.
