## Case definition

This dossier treats Colin Rosenblum and Samir Chaudry as two distinct people operating a collaborative media system. It reconstructs their predecessor venture, current channels, newsletter, education products, live events, brand relationships, staff dependencies and institutional collaborations. It does not infer equal ownership, current cap-table positions, voting control, individual compensation, IP ownership, profitability, causal performance or psychology.

Proposed public headline: **Cofounders of a creator-focused media company spanning video, audio, newsletters, education and events.**

Role-built flag: `false`. The record supports founder, creator, host and company-building activity, not an existing institution designing unique employment roles around either person.

## Identity resolution

Colin Rosenblum and Samir Chaudry in this case are the U.S. creators who began working together in 2012, built The Lacrosse Network, later launched the Colin & Samir media properties and identify as cofounders of The Publish Company. The case does not merge their individual accounts, employees, guests, represented creators or unrelated people with similar names.

## Atomic claims

1. Rosenblum and Chaudry say they have worked together since 2012 and built and sold their first YouTube channel. This supports a long collaboration, not equal ownership or identical contribution. (CS-S01, S03)
2. Independent reporting documents The Whistle's 2014 acquisition of The Lacrosse Network. The acquired network, its staff and transaction are distinct from the later Colin & Samir channel and The Publish Company. (CS-S04)
3. After leaving the acquired operation in 2016, the pair reported several years of uncertain positioning, freelance production work and a 2019 loss of approximately $18,000. That figure is a first-person historical P&L statement, not audited current financial evidence. (CS-S05)
4. The pair attributed a 2020 Samsung annual brand-ambassador contract to creating financial room to focus their channel. A sponsorship contract is a commercial relationship, not ownership by Samsung or proof of later business outcomes. (CS-S05)
5. Their current system includes the Colin & Samir YouTube channel and show, The Publish Press, Creator Support, educational offerings and live Press Publish events. These are distinct products with different distribution, labor and counterparties. (CS-S01–S03, S07–S14)
6. The Publish Press identifies Colin and Samir as founders and now reports a three-times-weekly publication. Audience and archive counts are issuer-reported snapshots, not audited reach or evidence of ownership economics. (CS-S02)
7. Creator Support began as an audio-only weekly format before becoming a separate YouTube channel. Samir stated it initially had no brand partner, AdSense revenue or direct revenue plan. This is dated product-development evidence, not a current monetization finding. (CS-S07, S08)
8. Creator Startup was developed over roughly two years and released with Kajabi as a paid cohort product. The platform partner, curriculum contributors, payment flow and contract economics prevent treating it as solely owned or produced by either founder without further records. (CS-S09)
9. Whalar appointed Rosenblum and Chaudry co-chairs of The Lighthouse creator council and creators-in-residence. Those delegated roles belong to a Whalar-built program and do not establish ownership of The Lighthouse. (CS-S10)
10. Public sources identify Rosenblum and Chaudry as cofounders of The Publish Company, which houses media, education and event activities. Cofounder status does not disclose equity percentages, voting arrangements, entity boundaries or current governance. (CS-S09, S11)
11. The business depends on employees and collaborators. First-person material describes producers, editors, brand partners and a team; LinkedIn supplies only an issuer-controlled 2–10 employee-range snapshot. Neither supports a two-person or solo-labor model. (CS-S08, S15, S16)
12. Press Publish is a company event product involving speakers, attendees, venues and partners. Public attendance and performance descriptions remain attributed and do not establish profitability or sole founder causation. (CS-S12–S14)
13. The pair's shared public identity must not erase individual authorship, decision rights, labor, contracts or economic interests. Joint branding is not proof of a 50/50 partnership.
14. No reviewed public record establishes the current cap table, equal equity, voting control, board or manager structure, profit shares, salaries, sponsorship economics, ownership of channel accounts, newsletter list, show masters, trademarks, course materials or event IP.
15. No reviewed source measures either person's psychological constructs. Motivation, resilience, cognition, compatibility and mental state may not be inferred from interviews, business survival, output cadence or personal adversity.
