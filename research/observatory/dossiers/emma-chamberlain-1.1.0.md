## Atomic claims

1. Chamberlain Coffee Inc. is a Delaware corporation formed in 2019. The 2024 SEC filing identifies Emma Chamberlain as a director and promoter, not as an executive officer in that filing. (EC11-S06)
2. The filing identifies other related persons, including Christopher Gallant, Paul Yoo and Adam Kaller; the company cannot be modeled as a one-person legal or governance structure. (EC11-S06)
3. Company and interview sources attribute the brand’s origin to Chamberlain, but do not disclose the initial cap table, incorporation mechanics, voting rights, or sole ownership. (EC11-S01, S02, S17)
4. Chamberlain Coffee announced outside financing in 2022 and 2023; public announcements do not establish valuation, dilution, current ownership or investor control. (EC11-S02, S03)
5. The company launched Walmart-exclusive ready-to-drink lattes in 2023 and later reformulated them; distribution and iteration are documented, but sales and continuing exclusivity are not. (EC11-S04, S05, S15)
6. Chamberlain became co-CEO with Gustav Hossy in August 2024 after serving as chief creative officer and expanding into retail strategy. The title does not establish unilateral authority. (EC11-S07, S08)
7. Spotify announced a multiyear exclusive license for Anything Goes beginning in 2023, covering the back catalog and future episodes. A license is not proof that Spotify owns the underlying IP or that Chamberlain retained every right. (EC11-S09, S10)
8. Chamberlain announced an indefinite podcast pause in April 2026. The Spotify archive’s continued availability must not be described as continuing episode production. (EC11-S11, S12)
9. Investigative reporting based on investor materials says Chamberlain Coffee operated at a loss in 2024 and encountered fundraising difficulty. These are attributed reports, not audited public financial statements. (EC11-S13, S14)
10. Public evidence documents multiple institutions and counterparties—company directors and executives, investors, retailers, Spotify and brand collaborators—so “built entirely around Emma” is an interpretive hypothesis, not an observed fact. (EC11-S02–S16)
11. No reviewed source establishes Chamberlain’s current equity percentage, voting control, board majority, personal wealth, podcast economics, retailer economics, or causal responsibility for company outcomes.
12. No reviewed source measures psychological constructs; none may be inferred from publishing cadence, business changes, or first-person statements.

Issuer releases are one provenance cluster even when syndicated.
