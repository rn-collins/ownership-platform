## Contradictions, adverse evidence, and open questions

1. “Inventor of ImageNet” is common institutional shorthand, but the scholarly record requires team, coauthor, annotator, challenge-organizer and infrastructure attribution.
2. “Godmother of AI” is a media label, not a formal role or proof of authority over the field.
3. World Labs financing and reported valuation are company-level measures and do not establish Li’s personal ownership, proceeds or net worth.
4. Academic leadership, nonprofit chairmanship and startup CEO authority operate under different governance and conflicts systems.
5. Project Maven evidence must remain attributed and time-bounded; it does not establish secret intent, personal legal liability or psychology.
6. ImageNet’s downstream role in deep learning does not support attributing every subsequent computer-vision result or social consequence to Li.
7. World Labs’ products are institutional outputs with named cofounders, researchers, engineers, investors and partners.
8. Book authorship does not resolve publisher, audiobook, translation, adaptation or publicity rights.
9. Current World Labs and Stanford status requires periodic refresh.
