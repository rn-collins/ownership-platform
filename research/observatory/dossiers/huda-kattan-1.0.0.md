- **Primary correction:** Public sources support a return to full founder ownership and Kattan’s control. They do not disclose a post-transaction cap table proving that Huda Kattan personally owns 100% of every equity interest.

## 1. Defensible replacement

> Huda Beauty announced in June 2025 that Huda Kattan had repurchased TSG Consumer’s minority interest, returning the company to full founder ownership and Kattan’s control. The transaction followed Huda Beauty’s divestiture of KAYALI to Mona Kattan and General Atlantic. Deal values and the post-buyback allocation among founder holders were not publicly disclosed.

## 2. Entity boundaries

The following must remain distinct:

- **Huda Kattan:** individual founder, public creator and executive.
- **Huda Beauty:** the core cosmetics company/brand.
- **KAYALI:** fragrance business created within Huda Beauty and separated in 2025.
- **Mona Kattan and Alya Kattan:** publicly identified cofounders or founder-family participants in different sources.
- **TSG Consumer Partners:** minority investor from 2017 until the 2025 redemption/buyback.
- **General Atlantic:** post-separation co-owner of KAYALI with Mona Kattan.
- **HB Angels/HB Investments:** investment activity that should not be treated as Huda Beauty ownership.

## 7. Contradiction and uncertainty register

1. **“Huda owns it fully” is stronger than the evidence.** The company says “founder-fully-owned” and says Kattan regained full control. Those statements do not disclose whether Huda, Mona, Alya, a family vehicle or another founder holder owns particular percentages.
2. **Control and economic ownership are different.** Full control may derive from voting arrangements, executive authority or ownership. The governing documents are not public.
3. **Brand and legal entity may differ.** “Huda Beauty” is used as both a brand and company descriptor. The relevant legal entities and jurisdictions require resolution.
4. **KAYALI must be removed from the current Huda Beauty perimeter.** It became a separate company under Mona Kattan and General Atlantic.
5. **2017 valuation is historical.** It is not a present valuation, liquidation value, audited enterprise value or personal net worth.
6. **Reported sales are not necessarily company revenue.** Retail sales, net sales and GMV must not be interchanged.
7. **Founding dates conflict.** Sources cite 2013 for the Huda Beauty product company while a Reuters summary uses 2018 in a passage also discussing the sisters; event definitions require primary records.
8. **Independence does not mean absence of dependencies.** Retail distribution, manufacturing, debt, licensing, advisers and contractual commitments may remain material.
