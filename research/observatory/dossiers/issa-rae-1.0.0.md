## Case definition

Issa Rae is included as a creator-led institution case. The case concerns documented organizational formation, multi-vertical media infrastructure, creator and talent-development systems, platform partnerships, and investments. It does not measure personality, motivation, cognition, mental health, or private financial wealth.

Proposed public headline: **Creator, producer, and founder building connected media, talent, audio, and investment infrastructure.**

Proposed case type: `creator`  
Primary field: Media and entertainment  
Jurisdiction: United States  
Role-built flag: proposed `true`, subject to the limited language below.

Permissible role-built language: **Public records show that Rae founded or co-founded organizations spanning media production, talent management, audio, and other ventures. This supports describing her professional infrastructure as partly built around her creative work. It does not establish sole ownership, complete control, profitability, or independence from larger platforms.**

## Contradictions and limitations register

1. “Black-owned” and “woman-owned” are company self-descriptions; this dossier does not contain corporate ownership filings.
2. “Our Companies” establishes public architecture, not legal-subsidiary status or equity percentages.
3. The WarnerMedia announcement documents a major institutional partnership. It therefore qualifies any claim that Rae’s infrastructure is independent of established platforms.
4. Public sources reviewed here do not establish revenue, profit, valuation, headcount, cap tables, voting rights, or personal net worth.
5. Mission statements establish organizational intent, not measured effects.
6. Dates are omitted where the current primary source provides no date. Page-access dates are not treated as founding dates.
7. No psychological attribute is measured or inferred.
