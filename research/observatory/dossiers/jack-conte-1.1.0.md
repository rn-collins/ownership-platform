## Case definition

This version expands the immutable 1.0.0 record into a longitudinal public-evidence case spanning Conte's creator work, Patreon's formation, formal governance, outside capital, product and fee decisions, acquisitions, creator-media collaborations, and repeated workforce reductions. It separates Conte's personal creative properties from Patreon, and it does not infer equity, voting control, private wealth, contract economics, causal performance, or psychology.

Proposed public headline: **Musician and Patreon cofounder operating across creator media and venture-backed platform infrastructure.**

Role-built flag: `false`. Conte co-founded Patreon and occupies conventional founder, director and chief-executive roles. The reviewed record does not show that an existing institution designed a unique professional role around him.

## Identity resolution

Jack Conte in this record is the musician associated with Pomplamoose and Scary Pockets and the cofounder and CEO of Patreon, Inc. This dossier does not merge other people named Jack Conte, employee statements not attributable to him, or creator accounts merely hosted by Patreon.

## Atomic claims

1. Patreon publicly traces its 2013 origin to musician Jack Conte's creator-monetization problem and identifies Sam Yam as cofounder. The narrative does not establish sole invention, sole causation or initial ownership. (JC11-S01, S11, S12)
2. A 2021 Form D identifies Patreon, Inc. as a Delaware corporation and identifies Conte as executive officer, director and promoter. Formal status does not establish current equity, voting control, compensation or unilateral authority. (JC11-S03)
3. The same filing identifies Sam Yam as director and promoter and identifies Daniel Rimer, Dick Costolo, Josh Miller, Goli Sheikholeslami and Anne Raimondi as directors. Conte's CEO title therefore cannot be modeled as a one-person governance structure. (JC11-S03)
4. Patreon announced outside financing in 2019 and a $155 million Series F in 2021. The 2021 issuer release and Reuters reported a $4 billion valuation for that transaction; this is not Conte's personal wealth or Patreon's current value. (JC11-S02–S05)
5. Patreon proposed and then reversed a 2017 fee change after creator and patron backlash. The reversal documents executive accountability and product-governance failure, not a stable personal trait. (JC11-S06)
6. Patreon reduced five security positions and later 80 additional positions, about 17% of staff, in September 2022; reporting also documented Berlin and Dublin office closures. These events qualify a growth-only narrative. (JC11-S07, S08)
7. Patreon acquired Moment in 2023 to add ticketed digital experiences. The acquisition is a corporate transaction; public sources do not attribute the acquired product, team or IP solely to Conte. (JC11-S09, S10)
8. In July 2026 Conte announced a further reduction of 93 roles, about 20% of staff, and organizational flattening. The stated rationale remains a company account rather than independently proven causation. (JC11-S13)
9. Conte currently describes himself as half of Pomplamoose and Scary Pockets as well as Patreon CEO and cofounder. Those creator collaborations are distinct from Patreon and involve other creators, including Nataly Dawn and Ryan Lerman. (JC11-S14–S16)
10. Dead Wax publicly identifies Conte and Ryan Lerman as hosts and lists additional production contributors. It is a collaborative media property, not evidence of Conte's sole IP ownership or labor. (JC11-S15)
11. Patreon's current product strategy extends beyond payment processing into discovery, feeds, media and community functions. Product expansion does not establish successful outcomes, creator welfare or sole executive causation. (JC11-S11, S17)
12. Role-built status is false: the evidence supports creator, cofounder, director and CEO roles, but not an institutionally invented unique role.
13. No reviewed public record establishes Conte's current equity percentage, voting rights, board control, liquidation position, compensation, personal wealth, ownership of each creative property, or Patreon profitability.
14. No reviewed source measures psychological constructs. Motivation, resilience, cognition, identity and mental state may not be inferred from creator narratives, interviews, fundraising or workforce decisions.
