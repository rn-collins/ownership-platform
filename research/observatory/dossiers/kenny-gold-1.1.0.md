## Case definition

This case examines Kenny Gold’s movement through newly created social- and creator-economy leadership roles at Deloitte Digital and Edelman, the practices and knowledge products associated with those roles, and the limits of treating executive-title novelty as proof of ownership, institution-level firstness, or measurable commercial outcomes.

Proposed headline: **Agency executive appointed to successive newly created social and creator leadership roles at Deloitte Digital and Edelman.**

Proposed classification: professional institution-builder.  
Role-built flag: `false`. Employers created the roles; the reviewed record does not show that either institution or role was built around Gold personally.

## Atomic claims

### KG-C01 — Edelman appointment
Edelman appointed Gold Global Chief Creator Officer in June 2026.  
Permissible: **Edelman appointed Kenny Gold Global Chief Creator Officer in June 2026.**  
Restriction: appointment alone does not establish tenure, compensation, performance, control or ownership.

### KG-C02 — exact firstness boundary
Edelman describes the position as its first-ever Global Chief Creator Officer and a newly created role.  
Permissible: **Gold is Edelman’s first publicly announced Global Chief Creator Officer.**  
Prohibited: **Gold is the world’s or the agency industry’s first Global Chief Creator Officer.**  
Restriction: “global” is part of the title and remit; it does not expand the firstness population beyond Edelman.

### KG-C03 — Edelman remit and reporting line
Edelman assigns Gold global leadership of Edelman Creator, integration across creator, social, paid and performance, and a reporting line to Tristan Roy, President of Integrated Solutions and Delivery.  
Permissible: **Edelman’s announcement gives Gold a global creator mandate spanning social, paid and performance and identifies his reporting line.**  
Restriction: announced remit is not evidence of budget, hiring authority, P&L control, veto rights or delivered results.

### KG-C04 — existing institutional foundation
Gold stated that he was joining an existing foundation and team. Edelman reported approximately 200 creator specialists globally.  
Permissible: **Gold joined an existing Edelman creator organization rather than founding it from zero.**  
Restriction: the 200-person figure is employer-reported and does not mean 200 direct reports.

### KG-C05 — Deloitte appointment

## Contradiction, adverse-evidence and limitations register

- The seed phrase “first-ever Global Chief Creator Officer” is ambiguous. Evidence supports first at Edelman, not first worldwide or across agencies.
- Employer statements that Gold “built the practice from the ground up” remain attributed because no independent operating dataset establishes baseline, scale, revenue or causation.
- Gold joined an existing Edelman creator organization; the role was newly created, but the capability was not.
- “Approximately 200 creator specialists” is not a direct-report count.
- #DistanceDance was collaborative. Neither campaign conception nor results may be assigned solely to Gold.
- Research contribution does not imply sole authorship or academic validation.
- Successive roles are observable; motive, personality and psychology are not.
- No source establishes personal ownership of employer practices, underlying client IP, equity compensation or decision rights beyond announced mandates.
- Current remit should be checked after Edelman organization changes.
