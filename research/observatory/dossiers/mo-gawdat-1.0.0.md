## Identity resolution

Mohammad “Mo” Gawdat is the former Chief Business Officer of Google X, an author, podcast host, speaker, founder of the One Billion Happy mission, and co-founder of Unstressable LLC. This dossier does not merge those roles or infer that the associated organizations, media properties, books, commercial services, intellectual property, audiences, or revenues are personally or wholly owned by him.

## Seed hypothesis tested

The map describes Gawdat as a former Google X CBO, author, and participant in AI work. Public evidence supports those broad role families, but requires distinct institutional, contractual, co-author, production, platform, and claim-quality boundaries. It does not establish a single “Mo Gawdat enterprise,” sole creation, complete ownership, scientific validation of happiness claims, or predictive accuracy for his AI forecasts.

## Contradictions, adverse evidence, and open questions

1. “Google executive,” “Google X CBO,” “engineer,” and “AI expert” recur with inconsistent precision. Primary employment/title records have not been located.
2. First-party and commercial biographies variously claim 20+ co-founded businesses, board service, and large operational contributions. The underlying entity list, dates, ownership, governance, exits and failures are not disclosed.
3. One Billion Happy shifts between movement, mission and current website/product framing. Legal identity, financing, governance, reach and measured outcomes remain unresolved.
4. Flight Story and Emma.Love role descriptions are present in event and speaker biographies, but primary appointment, corporate and cap-table evidence remains incomplete.
5. AI timelines and labor forecasts are predictions. They must be dated, attributed, compared with contrary evidence, and never converted into established causal facts.
6. Happiness, stress, neuroscience and mental-health language requires a separate evidence-quality and safety review before any clinical or scientific endorsement.
7. No reviewed evidence establishes personal net worth, venture value, revenue, speaking income, book proceeds, charitable disbursement, or portfolio performance.
