## 1. Identity resolution

Jimmy Donaldson is publicly known as MrBeast. This dossier treats “MrBeast” as the public creator identity and Jimmy Donaldson as the individual. Beast Industries Co., Feastables, Beast Investments LLC, and the MrBeast Burger licensing venture are distinct legal or operating contexts and must not be collapsed into the individual.

## 2. Defensible replacement

> Business Insider reported that Donaldson testified in a November 2024 deposition that he owned “a little over half” of Beast Industries. Separate reporting described a 2024 financing at an approximately $5 billion valuation. Later outside investment could have diluted individual holdings, and no current public cap table or voting-rights schedule was located.

This wording is permissible only with the attribution, date, financing context, and current-cap-table limitation intact.

## 7. Contradiction and uncertainty register

1. **“Owns ~half” versus “a little over half.”** The latter is an attributed quotation tied to November 2024, not a current cap-table fact.
2. **$5 billion company versus financing valuation.** A private-round valuation is not audited enterprise value, market capitalization, liquidation value, or Donaldson’s net worth.
3. **Economic ownership versus control.** Multiple share classes and investor rights mean percentage economics cannot establish voting or board control.
4. **Primary versus secondary sale.** BitMine’s transaction combined issuer preferred shares and secondary common shares; dividing transaction value by approximate percentage does not yield a clean valuation.
5. **Brand versus entity.** “MrBeast” is not interchangeable with Beast Industries, Feastables, Beast Investments, or licensed operators.
6. **Allegations versus findings.** Complaints, denials, internal investigations, employment actions, and adjudicated facts must remain separate.
