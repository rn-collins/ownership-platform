## 1. Governing correction

The public record still does not support the present-tense map claim “Owns ~half of Beast Industries (~$5B).” The narrow, defensible statement remains that Business Insider reported Donaldson said in a November 2024 deposition that he owned “a little over half” of Beast Industries. That dated reported testimony is not a current cap table.

The expanded primary record now establishes that Beast Industries Co. is a privately held Delaware corporation; it had Series C preferred, Class A common, and transferable Class B common equity; transferred Class B shares automatically converted to Class A; and BitMine’s January 2026 primary-plus-secondary investment did not confer control or significant influence. None of those facts reveals Donaldson’s present fully diluted economic interest, voting power, board rights, proceeds, or net worth.

## 5. Provenance and entity treatment

- The Form 10-Q and its XBRL note are one primary filing cluster.
- Later reproductions of the filing are derivative and do not add independent corroboration.
- “Existing shareholder” is intentionally unnamed in the filing; the transaction cannot be attributed to Donaldson.
- Class B-to-Class A conversion establishes class differentiation, but the public text reviewed does not disclose the voting ratio or all preferences.
- “Corporate parent” in one federal disclosure does not prove that Beast Industries Co. owns every MrBeast-branded asset, channel, trademark, contract, or affiliate.
- Beast Industries Co., MrBeastYouTube LLC, Beast Investments LLC, Feastables, Viewstats, Lunchly, Beast Games, licensed food operations, and prospective businesses must remain separately modeled.
