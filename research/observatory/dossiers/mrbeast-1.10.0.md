## 1. Governing correction and extension

Destiny Tech100 Inc.'s filed 2025 annual report and Form N-PORT establish a third disclosed outside institutional position in Beast Industries, separate from the later BitMine and Eightco positions. Destiny reported 372,578 Beast Industries Series C preferred shares acquired October 28, 2025, at cost of $15,299,990 and fair value of $14,999,990 as of December 31, 2025.

The Form N-PORT independently reports 372,578 shares, value of $14,999,990.28, 3.4243098194 percent of Destiny Tech100's net assets, a long payoff profile, preferred-equity classification, restricted-security status, and Level 3 fair-value classification. These are Destiny Tech100 portfolio-accounting facts. They do not establish the percentage of Beast Industries owned, current fair value, company valuation, voting or board rights, seller identity, Donaldson's dilution, or personal proceeds.

## 3. Preserved boundaries

1. A fund's percentage-of-net-assets figure is not the investor's percentage ownership of the portfolio company.
2. Reported cost, fair value, carrying amount, current market value, and company valuation are separate constructs.
3. Level 3 classification reflects significant unobservable valuation inputs; it is not proof of error, impairment, fraud, or current value.
4. Restricted-security status does not disclose all transfer restrictions, side letters, voting rights, information rights, board rights, or disposition rights.
5. Destiny Tech100's position is distinct from BitMine's and Eightco's positions.
6. No reviewed source identifies the seller, transaction proceeds recipient, Donaldson's dilution, Donaldson's beneficial ownership, or his personal economics.
7. Prior versions remain immutable. Version 1.10.0 adds an earlier institutional-investor position; it does not alter prior claims.
