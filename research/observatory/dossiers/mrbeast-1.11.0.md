## 1. Governing extension

Destiny Tech100 Inc.'s May 12, 2026 prospectus supplement reports that, as of March 31, 2026, its Beast Industries Series C preferred-stock position represented 2.0 percent of Destiny's approximately $742.5 million portfolio. This extends the December 31, 2025 position captured in version 1.10.0, but the supplement does not disclose a revised share count, a position-specific dollar fair value, a disposition, impairment, Beast Industries ownership percentage, or rights attached to the shares.

BitMine's July 20, 2026 SEC-filed investor communication reports that, as of July 19, 2026, it continued to describe its Beast Industries stake as $180 million. This extends the dated reporting chronology but remains company shorthand for BitMine's position; it is not a current independent fair-value determination, Beast Industries valuation, ownership percentage, or evidence of Donaldson's personal economics.

## 3. Preserved boundaries

1. Destiny's 2.0 percent is a percentage of Destiny's portfolio, not percentage ownership of Beast Industries.
2. The March 31 portfolio table does not itself disclose a revised share count or exact position-specific fair value.
3. A percentage multiplied by an approximate portfolio total would be an analyst-derived estimate, not a filed position value, and is not treated as canonical here.
4. BitMine's $180 million description is distinct from purchase consideration, capitalized transaction costs, carrying amount, impairment status, current fair value, and company valuation.
5. Later repetition of a reported stake amount does not prove no intervening transaction or unchanged legal rights.
6. No reviewed source resolves the complete cap table, investor voting or board rights, seller identity, Donaldson's dilution, beneficial ownership, voting control, transaction proceeds, or personal economics.

## 5. Provenance and accounting treatment

- Both records are public SEC filings by the reporting investors and are primary evidence of those investors' own dated descriptions.
- Destiny's March 31 portfolio percentage extends the position chronology but is not a Beast Industries ownership percentage.
- BitMine's July 19 stake description extends its reporting chronology but is not independently audited current fair value or a Beast Industries valuation.
- The two measures are not combined because their measurement bases, issuers, dates, and reporting frameworks differ.
