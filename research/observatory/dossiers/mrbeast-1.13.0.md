## 1. Governing extension

Destiny Tech100's May 26, 2026 Form N-2 registration statement describes Beast Industries Co. as a North Carolina-based entertainment and consumer-products company built around MrBeast. The filing says the company extended its brand into consumer packaged goods and was expanding into branded digital services, framing this as a transition from monetizing attention toward building large-scale brand-driven enterprises.

This is a public-company investor's filed description of a portfolio company. It is evidence of Destiny Tech100's disclosed understanding, not an audited Beast Industries segment report, an admission by Beast Industries or Donaldson, proof that each described service launched, proof of revenue, or evidence of Donaldson's personal ownership, control, proceeds, or wealth.
