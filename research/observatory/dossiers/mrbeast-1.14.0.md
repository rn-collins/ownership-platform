## 1. Governing extension

BitMine's filed investment note reports that on January 15, 2026 it acquired 3,974,167 Beast Industries Series C preferred shares at $40.26 per share and 709,672 common shares in a secondary transaction. The common shares had been Class B and automatically converted to Class A upon transfer. BitMine reported that the combined position represented approximately 4% of Beast Industries' equity at the time of the transaction, without control or significant influence. It also reported a side letter permitting Beast Industries, for a limited period, to offer up to $20 million of additional preferred equity at the Series D price.

These are BitMine-reported acquisition and accounting facts. They do not establish Donaldson's ownership percentage, dilution, voting control, seller identity, personal proceeds, current value, or current cap table.
