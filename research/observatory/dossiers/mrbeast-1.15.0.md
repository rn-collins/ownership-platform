## 1. Governing extension

American Exceptionalism Acquisition Corp. A's filed prospectus describes Social Capital as an investor in Beast Industries Co. and lists Chamath Palihapitiya as a director of Beast Industries Co. The same filing characterizes Beast Industries as a media company and includes the investment among Social Capital investments it says had strong private-market value creation.

These are representations made in a third-party issuer's SEC filing. They establish filed relationship evidence but do not establish investment amount, security class, acquisition date, current holding, board appointment date, current board status after the filing, governance rights, compensation, complete legal name or form, Donaldson's ownership, or personal economics.
