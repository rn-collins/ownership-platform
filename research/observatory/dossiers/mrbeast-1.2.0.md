## 1. Governing correction

The public evidence now resolves one narrow IP question without resolving personal ownership. The federal MRBEAST registration began with MrBeastYouTube LLC as applicant and registrant. A recorded 2021 assignment transferred that registration to Beast Holdings LLC, which current trademark records identify as owner. A May 2026 federal Rule 7.1 disclosure separately identifies Beast Industries Co. as corporate parent of Beast Holdings LLC, GameChanger247 LLC, and MrBeastYouTube LLC.

Those records support an entity-level trademark and parent-subsidiary map. They do not establish that Jimmy Donaldson personally owns the MRBEAST registration, Beast Holdings LLC, the parent company, every related mark, every channel, or every item of associated goodwill. They also do not disclose intercompany licenses, consideration for the assignment, present beneficial ownership, voting control, board rights, or personal proceeds.

- Beast Industries Co. is modeled as the disclosed corporate parent of three named LLCs only.
- GameChanger247 LLC is added to the known subsidiary graph because the May 2026 disclosure names it.
- The Russell Brands case is evidence of parties, filings, and procedural posture; it is not evidence of infringement or final liability.
- Beast Holdings LLC, GameChanger247 LLC, MrBeastYouTube LLC, Beast Industries Co., Beast Investments LLC, Feastables, Viewstats, Lunchly, Beast Games, Step, and licensed operations remain separate until specific records connect them.
