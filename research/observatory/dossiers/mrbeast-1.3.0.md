## 1. Governing correction

The public capital record now includes a second separately disclosed outside investor. Eightco Holdings' filed first-quarter 2026 financial statements state that it acquired a non-controlling minority equity interest in privately held Beast Industries for total cash consideration of $17,999,952. Eightco committed up to $25 million, approximately $18 million was funded as of March 31, 2026, and the period to call the additional $7 million expired on May 9, 2026.

This evidence establishes Eightco's investor-specific cost and minority status. It does not disclose the acquired share class, number of shares, percentage ownership, seller or issuer, voting rights, board rights, information rights, current fair value, Donaldson's dilution, or Donaldson's personal proceeds.

A separate Eastern District of North Carolina docket identifies the entity as **GameChanger 24/7, LLC** and records its April 24, 2026 financial disclosure naming Beast Industries Co. as corporate parent. That spelling should replace the unverified `GameChanger247 LLC` shorthand used in 1.2.0 unless an original filing establishes a separate entity. The docket is procedural evidence, not proof of liability.
