## 1. Governing correction

The prior record established Beast Holdings LLC as the current registered owner of the central MRBEAST registration. The expanded public trademark record shows that Beast Holdings LLC is listed as owner or applicant across a much broader portfolio. Public listings include MRBEAST GAMING, MRBEAST BURGER, BEAST PHILANTHROPY, FEASTABLES MRBEAST, CREATORGLOBAL, BEAST GAMES, MRBEAST EN ESPAÑOL, TEAMWATER, VYRO, THE VIRAL COMPANY, BEAST MOBILE, MRBEAST MOBILE, BEAST MODE, BEAST ATHLETICS, WATCHTIME STUDIOS, BEAST PLATFORM, BEAST MEMBERSHIP, BEAST SOCIAL, and MRBEAST SOCIAL, among others.

This materially strengthens the entity-specific IP boundary: the public record points to Beast Holdings LLC, not Donaldson personally, as the named owner or applicant for many marks associated with the broader brand system. It does not establish that every application matured into registration, that each named concept launched or operates, that Beast Holdings owns every relevant mark or other form of IP, that intercompany licenses exist on any particular terms, or that Donaldson personally owns or controls Beast Holdings.

## 3. Preserved boundaries

1. Beast Holdings LLC remains distinct from Donaldson, Beast Industries Co., MrBeastYouTube LLC, GameChanger 24/7, LLC, Beast Investments LLC, Feastables, Viewstats, Lunchly, Beast Games, Step, licensees, and charity organizations.
2. A trademark application is not a registration, adjudication, launch announcement, operating entity, or proof of current use.
3. A registered mark is not proof of complete ownership of the associated business, product, content, copyright, patent, domain, data, or likeness rights.
4. Portfolio listings are useful discovery evidence but do not replace status review of each official USPTO record.
5. The approximate portfolio count reported by a third-party index is not treated as a canonical total until the official records are fully clustered and deduplicated.
6. Similar marks, class variants, design marks, word marks, and duplicate applications must not be collapsed without record-level review.
7. Filing intent does not establish commercial operation, revenue, valuation, causal performance, or market success.
8. No inference is made about Donaldson's beneficial ownership, voting control, compensation, personal proceeds, or net worth.
9. `roleBuiltFlag=false`; the trademark evidence does not establish that Donaldson constructed a novel company-specific role.
10. Publication, verification, scoring, psychology, and saturation decisions remain disabled.

- Named ownership or applicant status is attributed to Beast Holdings LLC exactly as displayed.
