## 1. Governing correction

Eightco's filed first-quarter investment note already established $17,999,952 funded, a commitment of up to $25 million, and expiration of the additional $7 million call period on May 9, 2026. A later SEC-filed July 9 holdings update reports only an $18 million funded Beast Industries investment as of July 8. Read together, those records support a bounded post-expiration conclusion: the reviewed public filings do not show the additional $7 million as funded under that disclosed commitment.

Several March–May SEC-filed press releases used inconsistent shorthand. Some headlines or summary language called the Beast Industries position a $25 million investment while the same release explained that only $18 million was funded and $7 million remained a future commitment. The filed financial-statement note and the later post-expiration holdings update are more precise evidence for funded cost. The inconsistency is retained as disclosure-quality evidence; it is not treated as fraud, accounting misstatement, rescission, or evidence about Donaldson's personal economics.
