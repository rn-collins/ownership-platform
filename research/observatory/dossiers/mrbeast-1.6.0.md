## 1. Governing correction

The official record confirms that Beast Holdings LLC is the named applicant in selected trademark matters, but portfolio ownership does not mean every application is registered, uncontested, commercially launched, or personally owned by Jimmy Donaldson. USPTO nonfinal office actions are examination-stage communications rather than final refusals. TTAB opposition records are adversarial proceedings rather than final findings of invalidity, infringement, liability, or marketplace confusion.

## 3. Preserved boundaries

1. An application is not a registration.
2. A nonfinal office action is not a final refusal or abandonment.
3. A TTAB opposition is not a final merits ruling or civil infringement judgment.
4. The reviewed records identify Beast Holdings LLC as applicant; they do not establish Donaldson's personal ownership, control, proceeds, or wealth.
5. Trademark filings do not prove that a named product or service launched or operates.
6. Each serial number, class, prosecution history, and proceeding remains application-specific.
7. The selected records do not complete official portfolio-wide clustering.
