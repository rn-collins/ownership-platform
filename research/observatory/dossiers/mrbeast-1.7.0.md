## 1. Governing correction

BitMine's public communications and financial statements use different measures that must not be collapsed. The January press release called the transaction a $200 million investment. The later filed financial-statement note reports approximately $160 million for Series C preferred shares plus approximately $20 million for secondary common shares, or approximately $180 million of cash consideration, and separately capitalizes $6.024 million of transaction costs to reach a $186.024 million carrying amount at February 28, 2026. A May 31 filed holdings update again called the stake $180 million.

These figures can coexist as differently defined company disclosures; the reviewed record does not establish fraud, impairment, rescission, a $20 million loss, current fair value, or Donaldson's personal proceeds. The earlier 1.1.0 statement that no follow-on purchase had occurred "by May 31, 2026" exceeded the cited 10-Q. The primary record supports that conclusion only as of February 28, 2026. The May 31 holdings summary does not independently resolve whether the side-letter right was later exercised, expired, amended, replaced, or remained open.
