Dossier 1.7.0 correctly concluded that its cited February-quarter Form 10-Q supported side-letter non-exercise only through February 28, 2026. A later BitMine Form 10-Q for the period ended May 31, 2026 now independently establishes that Beast Industries had not exercised its notification right and BitMine had not purchased additional preferred equity as of May 31. The later filing also reports a $186.024 million carrying amount at May 31 and no impairment for the nine months then ended.

This does not retroactively make the earlier citation sufficient. It supplies new primary support for the later endpoint. It does not establish expiration, exercise after May 31, amendment, replacement, current fair value, Donaldson's dilution, seller identity, personal proceeds, or personal ownership.
