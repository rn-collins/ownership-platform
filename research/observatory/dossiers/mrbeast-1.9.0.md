## 1. Governing correction and extension

The Russell Brands matter has progressed beyond the earlier pending-motion posture. On July 14, 2026, the Western District of Kentucky denied the superseded first motion to dismiss as moot, administratively remanded the renewed motion to dismiss pending limited jurisdictional discovery and supplemental briefing, required the parties to complete that discovery within sixty days, and referred pretrial matters to a magistrate judge. The order did not decide personal jurisdiction, failure to state a claim, infringement, liability, confusion, validity, damages, or any merits question.

Separately, the USPTO record for MRBEAST serial 99361495 now reports registration on July 14, 2026 for footballs. That status change establishes a federal registration attributed to Beast Holdings LLC for the identified goods. It does not establish Donaldson's personal ownership, ownership of the footballs or an operating company, commercial success, freedom from challenge, infringement by another party, or the outcome of the Russell Brands case.
