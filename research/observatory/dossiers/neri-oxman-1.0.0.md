## Identity resolution

Neri Oxman is a designer, architect, researcher, former MIT professor, founder and former director of MIT Media Lab's Mediated Matter group, and founder of the New York design company OXMAN. She developed the Material Ecology framework and published the Krebs Cycle of Creativity. These roles and works span a university, a research group, a private company, museums, publishers, collaborators, fabricators, funders, patent assignees, and project-specific teams. This dossier does not assign MIT, Mediated Matter, OXMAN, museum collections, patents, commissioned works, publications, financing, or team output to Oxman personally without holder-specific evidence.

## Seed hypothesis tested

The map presents Oxman as an architect of a unified creative system. Public evidence supports repeated conceptual and institutional leadership, including founding Mediated Matter and OXMAN. It does not support a solitary-inventor account or prove that Material Ecology, the Krebs Cycle, all project assets, or the current company form one personally owned portfolio. Founding, directing, authorship, inventorship, beneficial ownership, institutional IP, museum custody, and distributed labor remain separate.

3. Founding OXMAN and reported family investment do not establish the complete cap table, governance arrangements or holder-specific rights.
4. Material Ecology can be attributed as Oxman's articulated framework without treating a scientific or design field as private property.
5. Patent inventorship, patent ownership, institutional assignment and commercialization economics are distinct.
6. Museum exhibition and collection records do not resolve title, copyright, fabrication credit or commercial value for every work.
7. Environmental and biological performance claims for OXMAN projects remain project- and evidence-specific.
