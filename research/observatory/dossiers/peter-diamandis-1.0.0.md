## Purpose

This dossier tests the seed hypothesis “engineer, physician and XPRIZE founder.” It separates education and credentials, nonprofit formation and governance, executive succession, co-founded companies, public-company directorships, venture funds, paid media and membership products, books, reported portfolio counts, institutional labor, adverse evidence and psychology.

## Identity resolution

The subject is Peter H. Diamandis, M.D., the MIT- and Harvard-trained founder and executive chairman of XPRIZE and co-founder of Singularity. XPRIZE, Singularity, SEC filings, Fountain Life and contemporaneous reporting align. No similarly named person was merged.

## Evidence-backed synopsis

Diamandis holds an M.D. from Harvard and engineering/science degrees from MIT. Public records support a long formation history including Students for the Exploration and Development of Space, International Space University, XPRIZE, Singularity, Human Longevity, Celularity, Vaxxinity-related predecessor ventures, Fountain Life and BOLD Capital Partners. Those relationships have different legal forms, dates, cofounders and governance systems.

XPRIZE identifies Diamandis as founder and executive chairman. In 2018, XPRIZE appointed Anousheh Ansari CEO while Diamandis retained the founder/executive-chairman role. XPRIZE competitions depend on benefactors, boards, staff, prize teams and outside funders. Announced competition totals and induced research-and-development estimates are institution-level or promotional figures, not Diamandis’s personal capital, income or causal output.

Singularity says it was founded in 2008 by Diamandis and Ray Kurzweil. A 2018 financing announcement moved Diamandis to “executive founder,” retained him on the board and documented separate capital and leadership. The current site identifies a team and operating institution. Founder status does not establish current ownership, unilateral control or sole authorship of its programs.

Human Longevity was co-founded with J. Craig Venter and Robert Hariri. Fountain Life identifies William Kapp, Tony Robbins, Robert Hariri and Diamandis as founding partners and Kapp as CEO. BOLD identifies Diamandis as a co-founder and partner. Fund size, assets under management and portfolio-company values are not personal wealth or proof of ownership of portfolio companies.

SEC records provide unusually strong boundaries for Celularity and Vaxxinity. Celularity filings document Diamandis as a co-founder of the predecessor and a director, while also documenting a multi-person board and Robert Hariri’s executive leadership. Vaxxinity filings document a director/audit-committee role alongside CEO Mei Mei Hu and other directors. These records support formal influence while blocking sole-control, company-performance and personal-value inferences.

The commercial system also includes books, newsletters, speaking, podcasts and Abundance360, a paid membership/community product. These media and education properties can distribute Diamandis’s ideas and generate business opportunities, but public pages do not establish their complete legal ownership, IP schedules, revenue, staff contribution, cross-entity contracts or customer outcomes.

Adverse evidence is material. Independent reporting documented a January 2021 in-person Abundance360 gathering followed by at least 24 reported COVID-19 cases; Diamandis publicly acknowledged that the attempted “immunity bubble” failed and that he had made a mistake. This supports an attributed event and operational judgment correction, not a stable psychological trait. Human Longevity’s executive turnover and litigation involving co-founder Craig Venter likewise concern an institution and disputed allegations, not automatic personal fault by Diamandis.
