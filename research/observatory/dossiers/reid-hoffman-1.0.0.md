## Purpose

This dossier tests the seed hypothesis “founder, venture capitalist, author and podcaster.” It separates Reid Hoffman’s roles across LinkedIn, Greylock, Microsoft, Inflection AI, Manas AI, earlier PayPal and SocialNet work, investments, books, podcasts, nonprofit activity and political funding. It does not treat these properties as one company, one cap table, one operating team, one rights bundle or one personally owned portfolio.

## Identity resolution

The subject is Reid Garrett Hoffman, identified consistently in LinkedIn and Microsoft filings, Greylock and first-party biographies, and Manas AI records. The records connect him to LinkedIn as co-founder and former executive, Greylock as partner, Microsoft as a director through the 2026 annual meeting, Inflection AI and Manas AI as co-founder, and named media and publishing work. No similarly named person was merged.

## Evidence-backed synopsis

LinkedIn’s 2011 registration statement gives the strongest formation and role chronology. It identifies Hoffman as one of LinkedIn’s co-founders, a director from March 2003, CEO during two bounded periods, President of Products, Executive Chair and later board chair. LinkedIn was not a sole-founder or sole-labor company: public and institutional records identify Allen Blue, Konstantin Guericke, Eric Ly and Jean-Luc Vaillant as co-founders and document executives, employees, directors, investors and public shareholders.

LinkedIn’s 2016 merger proxy supplies a dated, security-specific beneficial-ownership snapshot. It distinguishes shares held by the Hoffman and Yee trust from securities for which Hoffman had voting or dispositive power without pecuniary interest. Microsoft’s announced all-cash acquisition was valued at approximately $26.2 billion. Neither the transaction value nor LinkedIn’s later membership, revenue or strategic value is Hoffman's personal proceeds, current wealth or sole-created value.

Microsoft appointed Hoffman to its board in 2017. In June 2026 Microsoft disclosed that he would not stand for re-election at the 2026 annual meeting and would continue until that meeting; it stated the decision was not caused by a disagreement over company policies or operations. “Microsoft director” is therefore current only through the documented transition. It is not ownership or executive control.

Hoffman’s current first-party biography identifies him as a Greylock partner and co-founder of LinkedIn, Inflection and Manas AI. Greylock fund assets, portfolio-company financing, investment returns and board seats belong to different funds, partners, limited partners, companies and security holders. An attributed investment count or portfolio valuation cannot be converted into personal ownership, liquidity or causal impact.

Inflection AI has especially clear rights boundaries. Microsoft’s 2024 and 2025 annual reports say Microsoft entered a non-exclusive license for Inflection IP, identify Hoffman as an Inflection co-founder and director, and state that a Hoffman-associated LLC and Greylock-affiliated entities each held less than 10% at the agreement date. The records do not reveal Hoffman's complete direct or indirect economics, the full cap table, license price allocation or current holding. They also preserve Mustafa Suleyman, Karén Simonyan, employees, investors and Inflection’s continuing corporate identity.

Manas AI launched in January 2025 as a company co-founded by Hoffman and physician-scientist Siddhartha Mukherjee. Its own announcement attributes work to an interdisciplinary team and states that it uses Microsoft Azure and Microsoft AI expertise. Reported seed financing is company capital, not Hoffman’s personal expenditure, compensation or company value. Drug-discovery goals and pipeline claims are prospective and do not establish approved medicines or patient outcomes.

Hoffman hosts or co-hosts Masters of Scale and Possible and co-authors books with named collaborators. First-party media pages describe Masters of Scale as a broader platform, while production, distribution, publishing, employee, guest and co-author rights remain separate. Hosting and co-authorship do not establish sole ownership of the media brands, recordings, feeds, books or derivative products.

Adverse evidence is material. Hoffman apologized for unknowingly funding a group connected to a disinformation effort in the 2017 Alabama Senate race and disavowed misinformation tactics. He also apologized for interactions with Jeffrey Epstein after Epstein’s conviction in connection with MIT fundraising. These are attributable events and acknowledgments, not adjudications of every allegation, proof of knowledge beyond the evidence, or psychological measurements.

## Contradictions and adverse evidence

1. “LinkedIn founder” shorthand can erase four named co-founders and institutional labor.
2. Historic LinkedIn beneficial ownership cannot be carried forward after the Microsoft acquisition.
3. The $26.2B acquisition value is not personal proceeds or current wealth.
4. Microsoft director status has a documented 2026 endpoint; the company reported no policy or operations disagreement.
5. Inflection’s Microsoft license was non-exclusive and involved separately identified holders, company IP and continuing corporate boundaries.
6. Manas’s scientific claims are prospective and depend on Mukherjee, an interdisciplinary team and Microsoft infrastructure.
7. Greylock investments and portfolio valuations belong to funds, partners, limited partners, companies and security holders.
8. Podcasts and books include producers, co-hosts, co-authors, publishers, platforms and other rights holders.
9. The Alabama and Epstein apologies prevent a success-only narrative but do not authorize unsupported intent, guilt or psychology claims.
10. No valid psychological measurement was located.
