## Identity resolution

Simon Cook is the current CEO of LIONS, the Informa business behind Cannes Lions, WARC, Effie, Contagious and Acuity Pricing. Public records distinguish this current group role from his earlier Cannes Lions awards, vice-president, managing-director and CEO roles. The record does not establish personal ownership of LIONS, Cannes Lions, their awards, brands, data, publications or commercial results.

## Seed hypothesis tested

The map identifies Cook as CEO of Cannes Lions. The current, more precise description is CEO of LIONS. His career chronology supports substantial responsibility for awards and organizational strategy, including credited work on the Tracks framework, but not personal creation or ownership of the festival, role, awards system or wider LIONS portfolio. Informa owns the business and places it within Informa Festivals. Juries, employees, entrants, partners and parent-company governance remain separately attributed. `roleBuiltFlag=false`.

## Contradictions, adverse evidence and open questions

1. “CEO of Cannes Lions” is historically accurate but less precise than the current “CEO of LIONS.”
2. Executive leadership does not establish personal ownership of LIONS, Cannes Lions or their brands.
3. Informa’s acquisition value belongs to the corporate transaction and cannot be converted into Cook’s proceeds or wealth.
4. Credited architecture of Tracks does not prove sole creation, IP ownership or complete labor attribution.
5. Juries and jury presidents retain distinct judging authority and labor.
6. The 2025 withdrawals are material institutional adverse evidence, not proof of Cook’s personal misconduct.
7. The integrity framework is a response whose implementation and effectiveness require later evidence.
8. Cook’s Brazil representation acknowledgement is bounded to the stated jury-selection problem.
9. Reported entry decline does not establish a single cause or individual performance failure.
10. Compensation, employment terms, beneficial ownership, contracts, award and data rights, complete labor, audited LIONS-only performance, causality and psychology remain unresolved.
