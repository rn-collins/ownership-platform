## Case definition

This dossier reconstructs Steven Cliff Bartlett's public operating system across Social Chain, The Diary of a CEO, Flight Story Group, Flight Fund, thirdweb, company investments, endorsements and newer creator ventures. It separates founder branding from legal ownership, directorship, significant control, investment exposure, platform rights, distributed labor and reported valuations.

Proposed public headline: **Entrepreneur, investor and podcast host operating through distinct co-founded companies, investment relationships and media properties.**

Role-built flag: `false`. The evidence concerns founder, investor, director, host and media roles; it does not establish an institution creating a unique employment role around Bartlett.

## Identity resolution

Steven Cliff Bartlett is the British entrepreneur and podcast host born in Botswana in 1992. This case does not merge him with unrelated people named Steven Bartlett. It does not treat Social Chain AG, Flight Story Group Ltd, Flight Story Limited, Flight Fund, thirdweb, The Diary of a CEO, Huel, Zoe, Stan Store or portfolio companies as one legal enterprise.

## Atomic claims

1. Bartlett co-founded Social Chain with Dominic McGregor. Later Social Chain AG valuations, group-company counts and stock-market milestones cannot be converted into Bartlett's personal wealth, current ownership or sole creation.
2. Bartlett and McGregor publicly left executive roles in 2020. Later Social Chain performance or valuation therefore requires date-specific attribution and evidence of any retained economic interest. (SB-S17)
3. The Diary of a CEO is a media property hosted by Bartlett and produced through a team and platform relationships. Host and creator visibility do not establish sole labor, complete IP title or sole economic receipt. (SB-S08)
4. Flight Story Group's public materials describe a family of media, marketing, investment, talent and technology operations. Shared branding does not establish a single entity, identical cap tables or Bartlett's personal ownership of every unit. (SB-S03–S05)
5. Companies House records Bartlett as a person with significant control in Flight Story Group and records share-capital changes, a 2025 demerger, share repurchases and other holders. Significant control is a statutory threshold category, not proof of 100-percent ownership. (SB-S01, S16)
6. Companies House records Bartlett's appointment as a Flight Story Group director ending on 21 October 2025. Ending a directorship does not establish an exit from ownership, control, employment, branding or contractual relationships. (SB-S01)
7. Oliver Yonchev and other directors and officers appear in the Flight Story record. Bartlett's public prominence does not erase cofounders, executives, employees, contractors, guests or production labor. (SB-S01, S02, S04)
8. Flight Fund has been reported as a $100 million fund. A fund-size statement concerns committed or targeted investment capital, not Bartlett's personal assets, ownership percentage, deployed capital, fund performance or personal return. (SB-S05, S06)
9. Bartlett co-founded thirdweb with Furqan Rydhan; reported financing and a $160 million company valuation do not establish Bartlett's stake, voting control, liquidity or personal wealth. (SB-S03, S07)
10. Public descriptions of Bartlett as an investor, director, co-owner, ambassador or endorser identify different legal and economic relationships. Each portfolio company must be modeled separately and dated. (SB-S11, S12, S14)
11. The ASA found Huel advertising misleading because it omitted Bartlett's Huel directorship and financial interest; ASA guidance separately identifies him as a Zoe investor. These are regulator-supported relationship facts, not proof of stake size or company control. (SB-S11, S12)
12. Independent reporting and a BBC investigation identified harmful or unsupported health claims in selected podcast episodes; Flight Studio disputed the breadth and framing. Allegations, published claims, responses and adjudicated findings must remain separate. (SB-S09, S10)
13. Reported audience numbers, rankings, revenues, deal offers and valuations are source-defined and time-bounded. None proves personal income, profit, enterprise value, audience quality or Bartlett's sole causation. (SB-S08, S13)
14. No reviewed public record establishes the complete current cap tables, voting rights, board control, IP schedules, platform-account ownership, fund economics, carried interest, contract terms, personal wealth or consolidated profitability across Bartlett's system.
15. No reviewed source validly measures Bartlett's motivation, resilience, cognition, personality, mental state or other psychological constructs.

## Provenance and contradiction controls

Bartlett-controlled and Flight-affiliated materials form one issuer cluster. Companies House establishes what was filed, while warning that it does not verify filing accuracy. Media repetitions of a valuation, audience figure or biography are not independent corroboration when they trace to the same press release or interview. “Founded,” “co-founded,” “director,” “person with significant control,” “investor,” “co-owner,” “host” and “ambassador” are not interchangeable.

The Social Chain record contains conflicting shorthand about the entity valued, Bartlett's role at the valuation date and the economic meaning of later listings. The Flight Story record changes materially in 2025 through capital actions, a demerger and Bartlett's director termination. The dossier therefore prohibits static “owns an empire” language.
