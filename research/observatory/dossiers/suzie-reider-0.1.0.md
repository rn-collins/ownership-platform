## Case definition

Suzie Reider is included as a professional institution / role-formation case. The case examines documented formation and leadership of revenue organizations across YouTube, Waze, and Lyft. It asks whether a professional's authority is evidenced by institution-building rather than merely by a senior title. It does not infer personality, motivation, cognition, mental health, or private compensation.

Proposed public headline: **Revenue and media executive whose documented roles include building YouTube's revenue organization and leading Waze and Lyft business lines.**

## Classification review

The current provisional label “Founded YouTube's revenue org” is too absolute for public display without attribution. Until an original Google/YouTube source is obtained, use: **Institutional biographies credit Reider with founding YouTube's Revenue and Marketing Organization.**

The “role-built” flag should remain provisional. Evidence of institution-building is stronger than evidence that an employer designed a unique position around Reider.
