## Case definition

Suzie Reider is included as a professional institution-building and role-formation case. The defined case concerns documented formation and leadership of revenue, marketing, sales, and media systems across YouTube, Waze, and Lyft. It tests whether the record supports describing a professional as an institution builder rather than merely a holder of senior titles. It does not measure personality, motivation, cognition, mental health, private compensation, or personal ownership.

Proposed public headline: **Revenue and media executive credited with building commercial organizations across YouTube, Waze, and Lyft.**

Proposed case type: `professional`  
Primary field: Media and platform business  
Jurisdiction: United States  
Role-built flag: proposed `false` pending stronger evidence that a role was created or materially redesigned around Reider.

Permissible case language: **Public and institutional biographies document repeated formation or leadership of commercial systems across YouTube, Waze, and Lyft. The strongest source for the YouTube formation claim is Lyft's attributed career biography, corroborated by Middlebury. This supports studying Reider as an institution-building professional. It does not establish sole causation, ownership of employer assets, or that any position was uniquely designed around her.**

## Contradictions and limitations register

1. No original Google or YouTube organization chart, appointment release, or internal record was located for the Revenue and Marketing Organization claim.
2. “Founded” and “launched” remain attributed biographical claims and do not establish sole causation.
3. Several dates and role boundaries are unavailable in the reviewed public sources.
4. Senior titles establish role records, not measured performance or the division of authority among collaborators.
5. The record concerns employer-owned infrastructure; it does not establish Reider's ownership of the systems she helped build.
6. Evidence supports institution-building study more strongly than the claim that a unique role was built around Reider.
7. No psychological attribute is measured or inferred.
