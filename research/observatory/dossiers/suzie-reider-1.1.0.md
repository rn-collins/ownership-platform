## Case definition

This version expands the case from retrospective biographies into a longitudinal public record of Reider's commercial roles, stated operating methods, product work and institutional context across YouTube, Google, Waze and Lyft. It distinguishes contemporaneous records, first-person accounts, company claims, independent reporting and regulatory context. It does not establish personal ownership, private compensation, unilateral decision rights, causal responsibility for company results or psychological attributes.

Proposed public headline: **Commercial platform executive with documented leadership across YouTube, Google, Waze and Lyft.**

Role-built flag: `false`. Public evidence supports repeated work in emerging commercial functions, but does not establish that an employer created a unique role around Reider.

## Contradictions and unresolved questions

1. Public descriptions alternate among 17 and 18 years at Google; these may reflect source date or rounding. Preserve the source-specific wording.
2. Waze titles vary over time and by biography: head of ads North America, managing director global ads and Global CRO. Treat as a possible progression, not interchangeable synonyms, until exact dates are established.
3. Contemporaneous records strengthen the 2006–2007 YouTube chronology but do not independently prove the later phrase “founded the Revenue and Marketing Organization.”
4. Company product announcements during Reider's tenure do not establish individual authorship or causal performance.
5. Lyft Media existed and reported growth before her December 2024 start; pre-existing results must not be attributed to her.
6. No public evidence reviewed establishes personal equity, intellectual-property ownership, compensation, contractual rights or private decision authority.
7. No psychological characteristic is measured.
