## Research question

What does the public record establish about Suzie Reider's role in forming and leading commercial organizations at YouTube, Google, Waze and Lyft, and what remains attribution, synthesis or unavailable?

## Bounded conclusion

Contemporaneous 2006 reporting identifies Reider as YouTube's first chief marketing officer and says she joined to build its sales and marketing groups. Later records document advertising-sales leadership, Google integration, brand and industry-development roles, Waze advertising and revenue leadership, and Lyft Media and Lyft Business responsibility. This supports a professional institution-building case inside employer-owned platforms. It does not establish that Reider alone created YouTube monetization, owned the systems, controlled all decisions, caused company-level financial outcomes, or had a role uniquely built around her.

Role-built flag: `false`.

## Contradictions and adverse findings

1. Public biographies alternate between 17 and 18 Google years; preserve source-specific wording.
2. One 2006 outlet reported that YouTube had not confirmed the CMO appointment by press time.
3. Waze titles vary and should be treated as chronological progression, not synonyms.
4. The later phrase “founded YouTube's Revenue and Marketing Organization” is strengthened by contemporaneous reporting that she joined to build sales and marketing groups, but sole causation remains unsupported.
5. YouTube's advertising organization operated through teams and Google integration; individual authorship cannot be presumed.
6. Lyft Media pre-dated Reider's appointment.
7. Company and campaign metrics are not person-level performance measures.
8. No reviewed evidence establishes personal ownership, compensation, IP rights or unilateral authority.
9. No reviewed evidence measures psychology.
