## Research questions

1. How are responses distributed for each item, including floor, ceiling, missing, and not-applicable patterns?
2. Do items intended to reflect the same construct provide related but non-redundant information?
3. Which dimensional structures are consistent with the construct registry and observed ordinal responses?
4. What uncertainty surrounds reliability estimates for each preregistered scale or subscale?
5. Are there response-pattern differences that require item revision, routing, claim restrictions, or further fairness study?

No individual score, rank, diagnosis, eligibility determination, prediction, causal conclusion, or population prevalence estimate is authorized by this pilot.

## Decision rules

For each item choose one: retain; clarify; split; re-anchor; route; defer; remove. A decision must cite the construct definition, cognitive-interview evidence, pilot evidence, uncertainty, fairness implications, and permissible claim language.

Passing numerical thresholds never activates scoring automatically. Activation requires a dated methodology record, approved crosswalk, claim-language review, all machine-readable gates marked met, and an explicit human activation decision.

## Deviations and reporting

Every deviation receives a date, author, reason, affected analysis, timing relative to outcome inspection, and classification as confirmatory or exploratory. Results must distinguish preregistered from exploratory analyses.
