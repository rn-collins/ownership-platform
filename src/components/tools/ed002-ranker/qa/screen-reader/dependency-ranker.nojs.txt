  - region "What this tool shows and what it does not":
    - heading "What this tool shows and what it does not" [level=2]
    - heading "Shows" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: Your own list, sorted by how many of the four tests each dependency fails
      - listitem: The edition’s four layers and four tests in the edition’s words
    - heading "Does not show" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: A score, a weight or a risk rating. The sort counts unticked boxes and nothing else.
      - listitem: Private contract terms, ownership shares or bargaining power in any case the edition names. The edition says its sources do not establish them.
      - listitem: Whether a dependency is a problem for you. The tests ask questions and your answers stay yours.
